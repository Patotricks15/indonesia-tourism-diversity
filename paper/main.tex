\title{Diversity Re-ranking with MMR and a Greedy DPP for Indonesia Tourism Destinations}
\author{Patrick Gomes de Oliveira}

\begin{abstract}
We study deterministic diversity re-ranking of a classical collaborative
relevance score for Indonesia tourism destination recommendation. A
shrunk-cosine item-item kNN model produces a candidate pool, which is then
re-ordered by Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR) and, as an optional
extension, by greedy MAP inference over a Determinantal Point Process
(DPP). Both re-rankers operate on the same content-based similarity matrix
(destination category and city) and the same relevance scores, which
isolates the effect of the re-ranking strategy from the effect of the
underlying recommender. We measure the resulting trade-off between ranking
quality (NDCG, Recall, MAP) and list quality (intra-list diversity,
category coverage).
\end{abstract}
