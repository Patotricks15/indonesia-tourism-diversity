\section{Dataset}
The Indonesia Tourism Destination dataset (Kaggle ref
\texttt{aprabowo/indonesia-tourism-destination}) contains ratings (1--5
scale) from users over destinations across five Indonesian cities, each
destination labelled with a category (e.g. \emph{Bahari}, \emph{Budaya},
\emph{Cagar Alam}). Ratings carry no event timestamp. A rating of 4 or 5 is
treated as a positive; an iterative $k$-core filter ($k=3$) removes users
and destinations with too little history.
