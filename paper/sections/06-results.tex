Table~\ref{tab:results} reports both families of metrics on the synthetic
fixture used in continuous integration. MMR and DPP both increase
intra-list diversity and category coverage relative to plain relevance
ranking, at the expected cost in NDCG and Recall: diversifying a ranked
list necessarily displaces some of the most relevant candidates. DPP's
set-level formulation yields a comparable diversity gain to MMR while
retaining a slightly lower drop in ranking quality, consistent with its
joint rather than greedy pairwise optimisation.
