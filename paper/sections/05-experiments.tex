\section{Experimental protocol}
As in \texttt{restaurant-ease-recommender}, the dataset has no timestamp,
so we use a user-aware random holdout (10\% validation, 20\% test per
eligible user). We compare four rankers at a fixed candidate pool size of
50 and list length $k=10$: plain relevance, MMR ($\lambda=0.5$), greedy DPP,
and a non-personalised popularity floor. Ranking quality is measured by
NDCG@10, Recall@10, MAP@10 and catalogue coverage; list quality by
intra-list diversity ($1$ minus the mean pairwise content similarity within
a list) and category coverage (distinct categories per list, normalised by
$k$).
