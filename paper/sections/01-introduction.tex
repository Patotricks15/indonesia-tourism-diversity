\section{Introduction}
Pure relevance ranking tends to recommend near-duplicate destinations: the
top of a ranking over co-visitation similarity is frequently dominated by a
single popular category. This project implements two classical,
non-neural, deterministic re-ranking strategies that explicitly trade some
relevance for diversity: Maximal Marginal Relevance
\cite{carbonell1998mmr}, a greedy heuristic, and a greedy approximation to
Determinantal Point Process MAP inference \cite{kulesza2012dpp}, which
models the whole selected set jointly rather than greedily balancing one
item at a time.
